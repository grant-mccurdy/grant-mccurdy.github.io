    Methods and limitations

    LOGOS-MATH-V2, revision 5. Violin shapes use the report's fictional score distributions and ggplot2 Gaussian density estimates, trimmed to the observed range and normalized to equal width within each group. Boxes show quartiles and the median; whiskers follow the report's 1.5-IQR rule. Diamonds show means; circles show outliers. The course order is cross-sectional, not individual student growth. AP Statistics is a separate pathway.

    The 22 planned comparisons retain their original Holm families, including all 14 class comparisons in one family. The heatmap retains its separate 45-pair family. Filtering never recalculates tests. Pointwise 95% intervals are not Holm-adjusted simultaneous intervals: an interval can exclude zero while the adjusted comparison remains inconclusive. Student-level intervals can understate uncertainty when students share a class. Section-cluster sensitivity results are retained in the planned-comparison table; few clusters remain a limitation.

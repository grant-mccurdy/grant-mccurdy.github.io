Mathematics assessment

An original assessment connecting mathematical content, scoring definitions, and a browser-based practice experience.

My role
Founder and builder: assessment design, item metadata, scoring workflow, and review tooling.
Artifact
30 questions, 45 working minutes, plus five minutes of instructions.
Status

Open practice assessment

Design problem

A useful diagnostic needs a clear relationship between the questions, the skills they examine, and the interpretation of responses. A department report is only inspectable when its underlying assessment can also be reviewed.

As the founder of Logos Education Group, I developed the assessment and its connected workflow. My mathematics teaching and department leadership informed the content and review decisions; this is Logos development work, separate from earlier employer systems.

The assessment experience

The form includes 22 multiple-choice and eight numeric-response questions across Algebra, Advanced Math, Data, and Geometry. The interface provides answer entry, a timer, review navigation, mathematical figures, and a browser-calculated personal diagnostic.

Implementation decisions

  • One canonical form: LOGOS-MATH-V2 revision 5 is shared by the practice experience, the department report, and the Schools diagnostic fixture.
  • Explicit item metadata: content and skill definitions connect individual questions to reporting views.
  • Reviewable scoring: form identity and scoring definitions are retained through the reporting workflow.
  • Separated records: a visitor's practice responses do not enter the 500-student fictional department dataset.

The canonical form identity is recorded in the public assessment manifest.

Evidence and limits

The revision-5 review packet binds the 30 items and their published artifacts to the same form. This establishes traceability, not psychometric validity.

Independent educator and accessibility review, pilot timing, and difficulty validation remain pending. The assessment does not establish national norms, SAT-equivalent scores, mastery, placement decisions, or learning gains. Public answer keys make this a practice preview, not secure school administration.

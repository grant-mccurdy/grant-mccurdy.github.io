Background

Professional experience

At Pacifica Christian High School, I taught mathematics through AP level and served as Mathematics Department Head. I led readiness and growth analysis, data-quality controls, SAT/AP target mapping, and Canvas/LMS-informed reporting for instructional and department decisions.

I built Python applications in Google Colab integrating OpenAI and Canvas APIs for assessment creation, publishing, and teacher-reviewed grading. Implementation included staff onboarding and training.

I founded Logos Education Group and build its assessment, reporting, and school software workflows. This extends my mathematics teaching and department leadership into product development. The demonstrations use fictional data, not school or client deployment records.

The Assessment Analytics dashboard and Logos department report present the same synthetic scenario through interactive course, class, comparison, and content-area views. Supporting evidence documents methods and limitations.
